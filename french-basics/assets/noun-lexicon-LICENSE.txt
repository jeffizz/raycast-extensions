This dataset has been modified: all noun entries were extracted, ranked, linked with their gender counterparts, and converted to SQLite. The ranking combines OpenSubtitles, Wikipedia, and Gutenberg frequencies. The MIT license declared for source code does not replace the CC BY-SA license for this dataset. Upstream LeXiK incorporates other open lexical sources; see its licensing and attribution pages for details.
